Joel Lindstrom operates this invitation-only technology demonstration. Lori is an AI assistant that demonstrates exam-support messaging using fictional scenarios. This is not an official Prometric or American College of Cardiology service, and it does not register you for an actual exam or change a real appointment.

Messages and consent

Participants may receive automated welcome messages, registration confirmations, exam reminders, preparation guidance, test-center information and responses to their questions. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Participation is voluntary and is not a condition of purchase or access to the demonstration.

To request participation, use the optional consent form on the home page and send the resulting email to Joel, or email the displayed consent statement with your name and mobile number directly. Joel must receive and record consent before enabling messages. The public form is not an automatic subscription service.

Stop and help

Reply STOP to unsubscribe. A final opt-out confirmation may be sent. For help, reply HELP or email lindstrom.joel@gmail.com. You may also request that Joel stop messages by email. The demonstration sending number is +1 236-301-7516.

Availability and accuracy

Messages depend on registration approval, carrier delivery and service availability. Delivery may be delayed or unavailable; carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. AI responses can be inaccurate. Do not rely on this demonstration for real exam policies, accommodations, appointments or urgent assistance. Use your official exam provider for real requests.
