Privacy policy · Effective October 27, 2026

Your information in this demonstration.

This policy covers Joel Lindstrom’s invitation-only Lori SMS exam-support demonstration and this public information site.

Information used

If you participate, Joel receives your name, mobile number, consent statement and date, and the messages you exchange with the demonstration. Fictional candidate and exam details may be associated with test conversations. Do not send medical documents, payment details, passwords or other sensitive information.

How information is used

Information is used to record consent, send requested demonstration messages, answer questions, honor opt-out requests, and troubleshoot the demonstration. Messages may be processed by AI and reviewed by Joel.

Service providers and sharing

Twilio delivers SMS messages. Microsoft Dynamics 365, Copilot Studio and Power Automate process demonstration conversations and workflows. Consent emails are processed through Joel’s Gmail account. These providers process information to operate the service.

Mobile numbers, SMS opt-in data and messaging consent will not be sold or shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Retention and choices

Consent emails and demonstration records are retained for operation, troubleshooting and documenting permission. No automatic deletion period is currently configured. You may request access, correction or deletion by emailing Joel. Records needed to honor an opt-out or meet applicable obligations may be retained; provider backups and logs follow their own retention settings.

This website

GitHub Pages hosts this public website and may process technical request information such as IP addresses. This site adds no advertising trackers or analytics. The consent form does not send data to a website database or store it in your browser; it opens an email draft, which you choose whether to send.

SMS disclosures

Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to unsubscribe or HELP for help. Participation is optional.
